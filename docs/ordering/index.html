Which vertex comes next?

The magenta vertex is your start. Tap the rest one at a time. Every new vertex must be connected to at least one vertex already in your list. When all are in, we check your list.

Tap a vertex to add it to your list.

In symbols: find an ordering P1, P2, …, Pn such that every Pk is connected to at least one of P1, …, Pk−1.
